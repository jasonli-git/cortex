# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in Cortex.

## Pull requests are not accepted

Cortex does not accept outside pull requests. This is deliberate, not a matter of
bandwidth.

The project is licensed under the AGPL v3, and I am currently its sole copyright
holder. That distinction matters: a license is a grant made to *others*, not a
constraint on the author, so sole ownership keeps relicensing and dual-licensing
available later — for example to distribute Cortex through channels whose terms are
incompatible with the AGPL, such as the Apple App Store.

Merging an outside contribution would make that contributor a copyright holder in
their portion of the code, and any future relicensing would then require permission
from every contributor. A single merged patch can close that door permanently, so
the simplest way to keep it open is not to open it.

## What is welcome

- **Bug reports and questions** — please open an issue.
- **Forks** — the AGPL explicitly permits them. Fork freely, subject to the license
  terms, including the network-use provision in §13.

If you have something you would genuinely like to see upstream, open an issue first
and we can discuss whether a contributor license agreement makes sense.
